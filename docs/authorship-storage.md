> **Status:** spike done 2026-10-13, **A5 built 2026-10-13 on candidate A** (§8, as built) · Optimo and upstream WordCraft round trips run, also for the feature's own document (`f-feature`) · **Word round trip not run: Microsoft Word is not installed on this Mac** (harness ready, §7; run it on a Mac with Word) · Decisions: ADRs "A5 spike" and "Assistant A5" in `docs/decisions.md`

## 1. Question

Optimo keeps "this text was written by the AI (author, model), accepted at T" after a suggestion is
accepted (HANDOFF §10, V2–V4). How is that stored inside a `.docx` so that it

- survives Microsoft Word opening and saving the file, including edits around and inside the
  marked text;
- survives upstream WordCraft (`storytold/wordcraft` main) opening and saving it;
- stays invisible or at least unobtrusive to a person editing in Word;
- keeps the file valid OOXML.

Losing the marks is tolerable; a file Word or WordCraft refuses or repairs is not.

## 2. Candidates

| | Mechanism | Where the metadata lives |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Hidden bookmarks `_MarginAI…` around the AI runs (ECMA-376 §17.13.6; a leading `_` hides a bookmark from Word's Bookmark dialog) | Custom XML data part `customXml/itemN.xml` in our own namespace `urn:margin:authorship` (§11.3.5, with `itemPropsN.xml` and relationships), one `span` per bookmark: author, kind, model, acceptedAt, length, hash |
| **B** | Nothing in the body | Same part, spans anchored by paragraph index + character offsets + hash |
| **C** | Attributes in our namespace on each AI `w:r`, namespace listed in `mc:Ignorable` and `mc:PreserveAttributes` (ECMA-376 Part 3) | On the runs |
| **D** | Inline rich-text content controls (`w:sdt`, §17.5.2) with `w15:appearance="hidden"` ([MS-DOCX]) | `w:tag` of the control |
| **E** (added) | A hidden character style `MarginAI` on the AI runs | Same part as A, spans anchored by style |

Considered, not built: comments (visible balloons); leaving the suggestions as tracked changes
(HANDOFF's "cheaper first step": that is pending, not accepted, text); editing-permission ranges
`w:permStart/End` (meant for Restrict Editing, Word may shade them); `docProps/custom.xml`
properties (flat values, shown to the user under Properties › Custom, and no anchor, i.e. B).

## 3. Test set-up

- **Document.** Four paragraphs of our own text with three AI spans: one mid-paragraph, one
  whole paragraph, one across two runs with different formatting (plain + bold). Generated by
  this repo's docx writer; what the writer has no API for (our custom XML part, the ignorable
  attributes) is patched into the package afterwards. Output is byte-identical on every run.
- **Round trips.** Optimo (this branch) and upstream WordCraft (`storytold/wordcraft` main at
  `159b84f`, 2026-10-13, built from a throw-away worktree), each twice: `convert` (open + save)
  and `run … --save` with edits that hit every case the brief asks for: an insertion at the start of
  the document (shifts every offset), an insertion inside span 1 (`river` → `wide river`), a
  deletion at the start of span 2 (`Seewis `), typing right after span 3 (` AFTER`).
- **Word.** Not run. There is no Word bundle in `/Applications`, Launch Services has no
  `com.microsoft.Word`, and `osascript` cannot reach it; only a Microsoft AutoUpdate remnant is
  left. The AppleScript round trip is written (open → scripted edits through Word's object
  model → save as .docx → close; no keystrokes, no clicking) and wired into `run.sh`; it runs as
  soon as Word is installed. The Word columns below are **expectations** from the OOXML spec and
  Microsoft's published bookmark notes, not measurements.
- **Validity.** `authorship_spike check` parses every XML and `.rels` part, verifies that every
  part has a content type, that every internal relationship target exists, that bookmark ids are
  unique and no start or end is orphaned. Every file in every stage: `issues=0`.

### 4.2 What the edits did (Optimo and upstream, identical)

| Span | Accepted text | After the edits (A, bookmark) | After the edits (D, control) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Mist lifts off the river by eight,` | `Mist lifts off the wide river by eight,` (edit inside, hash mismatch) | same |
| 2 | `Seewis sits above the Prättigau, …` | `sits above the Prättigau, …` (first word deleted, hash mismatch) | same |
| 3 | `pack a map and good boots` | `pack a map and good boots AFTER` (typed at the end went inside) | same |

Typing at the end of a span extends it in WordCraft's editor (for both A and D). That is editor
behaviour Optimo controls: on accept, the AI span is a bookmark, and Optimo's insert at a span's
end must go after the `BookmarkEnd` object, matching Word's documented rule. Upstream has no
reason to care.

## 5. Recommendation: A, hidden bookmarks + custom XML part

- It is the only candidate that is kept by both editors **and** keeps per-span identity **and**
  needs no change to the docx crate (here or upstream): the two pieces it uses already
  round-trip.
- Word's documented bookmark behaviour is what we want: edits inside stay AI, text typed after
  the closing bracket is the person's, partial deletes keep the mark, copies do not carry it.
- Invisible by default, no right-click affordance, no Developer-tab presence (unlike D).
- Readers other than Word and WordCraft (Pages, LibreOffice, Google Docs) keep bookmarks and
  ignore custom XML parts; the worst case is losing the marks, never a refused file.

**Risks and mitigations**

1. **Not measured in Word on this machine.** Run `scripts/authorship-spike/run.sh` on a Mac with
   Word before building A5's persistence (a one-time macOS automation prompt will appear the first
   time; refusing it fails the run with error -1743). If Word drops bookmarks in a flow we care
   about, D is the fallback.
2. **Over-attribution after a person edits inside a span.** The bookmark keeps the edited text.
   Mitigation: store the accepted text in the part (not just its hash) so Optimo can diff on load
   and keep only the unchanged characters as AI (§6). Cost: the AI text is in the file twice; cap
   it (hash only above ~10,000 characters per span).
3. **Name clashes.** Pasting between two Optimo documents can bring a second `_MarginAI1`. Use
   unique names (`_MarginAI_<8 hex>`), and keep the first on load.
4. **Bookmark churn.** Hundreds of small spans are fine; coalesce adjacent spans with the same
   author on accept, and drop collapsed ones on save.
5. **A person who shows bookmarks sees grey brackets.** Accepted: it is an opt-in Word setting,
   and the names say what they are.

## 6. What Optimo must do on load (reconciliation)

The model, not the part, is the source of truth; the part is rewritten from the model on save.
On load, for `customXml/item*.xml` with root `mg:authorship` (`urn:margin:authorship`):

| Found | Do |
|---|---|
| Span entry, bookmark present with start and end in one story, start ≤ end | Keep. Compare the text with the stored text/hash: equal → AI as stored; different and text stored → diff (bounded, character-level), attribute only the common characters to the AI; different and hash only → keep as AI, flag `edited` |
| Span entry, bookmark missing (Word dropped it, or the text was deleted) | Drop the entry, log one line |
| Span entry, bookmark collapsed (start == end) | Drop entry and bookmark |
| Bookmark start without end, or end without start (the reader already skips ends whose id has no start) | Drop entry and bookmark |
| Bookmark end before its start, or crossing into another story / table cell | Drop entry and bookmark |
| Two bookmarks with the same name | Keep the first, drop the entry for the second, rename on save |
| `_MarginAI…` bookmark with no entry | Stale: remove on save |
| Span containing tracked changes | Characters in a tracked deletion are not counted; a person's tracked insertion inside the span is the person's |
| Overlapping or nested AI spans (accepted twice) | Merge |
| Part malformed, unrelated, or another store's root | Ignore; `keep_custom_xml` already carries foreign stores through unchanged |

On accept: wrap the accepted insertion in a new bookmark, record `{author, model, acceptedAt,
text}`; coalesce with an adjacent AI span of the same author in the same paragraph. On insert at
a span's end: place the text after the `BookmarkEnd` object.

## 8. As built (A5, 2026-10-13)

Candidate A, exactly as recommended, with the open question of §5 decided: **the accepted text is
stored** (hash only above 10,000 characters per span).

### 8.1 Model

- `Document::authorship` (`crates/doc/src/authorship.rs`): one `Span` per mark — bookmark name
  `_OptimoAI_<8 hex>`, `author` **as written on the accepted change** (never normalised; it may be
  a model's display name such as `AI · Mistral Large 4`), `model` (the id configured in Settings ›
  Assistant when the change was accepted; empty when unknown, e.g. an outside assistant),
  `acceptedAt` (ISO-8601 UTC), `text` (the accepted text, see 8.3) or, above the cap, only
  `hash` (FNV-1a 64) and `len`.
- The bookmarks are ordinary `InlineObject::BookmarkStart/End` markers in the paragraphs; the
  paragraphs are the source of truth. Spans are per accepted insertion, never across a paragraph
  on creation (Word or a person pressing Enter inside one can make them span two paragraphs
  later; that stays valid).
- Who is an assistant is decided in **one place**: `Session::is_assistant_author`, over the list
  `Session::assistant_authors` (the configured name, synced from Settings › Co-writer, plus every
  name an assistant has written under in the session — `Session::enter_assistant` records it) with
  one rule, `authorship::name_matches` (trimmed, case-insensitive). The UI's
  `review_panel::is_assistant` uses the same rule over the same list. Changing how assistants
  are recognised means changing those two bodies and nothing else.

### 8.2 Accept, reject, typing

- **Accept** (`review.accept`, `review.acceptAll`, by author, from the Review panel or the
  Assistant panel — all go through `cmd::review::resolve_para`): before a paragraph range is
  accepted, every assistant insertion in it (tracked `ins` whose revision author is an
  assistant, not deleted again, with visible text) is wrapped in a new mark
  (`engine::authorship::wrap_before_accept`); after the accept the text inside is recorded
  (`record_accepted`). A new span touching or inside an **unedited** span of the same author in
  the same paragraph extends that span (its stored text becomes the new whole; `acceptedAt` and
  `model` are refreshed); if the existing span was edited by a person, the new one stays its
  own, so the person's letters are never re-attributed to the AI. Undo takes marks and entry
  back with the accept.
- **Reject** leaves nothing: rejected insertions are removed, no mark, no entry.
- **Typing** (Word's rule for bookmarks, `docs/typing-parity.md`): text typed, pasted as text or
  split with Enter at the end of a span (the caret before its bookmark end) goes **after** the
  bookmark end — a person's continuation is the person's. Text typed right after the opening
  marker or inside stays inside (and counts as the person's through the diff, 8.4). Only
  `_OptimoAI_` bookmarks get this; ordinary bookmarks behave exactly as upstream
  (`typing_at_the_end_of_an_ordinary_bookmark_still_extends_it`).
- **Outside assistants** cannot forge or remove marks: `insert.bookmark` with a `_OptimoAI`
  name is on the deny list (`assistant::denied`); their accepted changes count as AI because the
  author on the change is an assistant's name. `authorship.spans` / `authorship.shares` are
  read-only and open to everyone.

### 8.3 File format (`.docx`, `.docm`, `.dotx`, `.dotm`, Flat OPC `.xml`)

Written by `crates/docx/src/authorship.rs` **only while the document has at least one span with
a live bookmark**; a document without them is written byte for byte as before (test
`a_document_without_spans_writes_no_part_and_a_plain_file_is_untouched`).

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| `customXml/itemN.xml` | `<mg:authorship xmlns:mg="urn:optimo:authorship" mg:version="1">` with one `<mg:span mg:anchor="bookmark" mg:ref="_OptimoAI_…" mg:author="…" mg:kind="ai" mg:acceptedAt="…" mg:model="…" mg:len="…" mg:hash="…"><mg:text>…</mg:text></mg:span>` per mark, in document order; `mg:text` is left out above the cap |
| `customXml/itemPropsN.xml` | `ds:datastoreItem` with a fixed `ds:itemID` `{6D1F3B2A-0C4E-4F8B-9A7D-2E5C1B3F4A60}` and a `ds:schemaRef` to the namespace |
| `customXml/_rels/itemN.xml.rels` | the item → its properties part (`customXmlProps`) |
| `word/_rels/document.xml.rels` | `customXml` relationship `../customXml/itemN.xml` (related from the main document, as Word relates its own stores) |
| `[Content_Types].xml` | overrides for both parts (`customXml+xml`, `customXmlProperties+xml`) |

`N` is the first free item number: the bibliography keeps `item1`, foreign stores carried through
from the opened file keep their names, ours is added beside them (test
`ours_sits_beside_a_bibliography_and_a_foreign_store`). The stored text goes through `storable`
(object placeholders removed, control characters other than tab and line feed replaced by U+FFFD)
so the part is always well-formed; the live text is put through the same function before it is
compared. A second save of an unchanged document writes identical bytes.

**Privacy.** The part holds the bookmark name, the author name as written, the model id, the
time of acceptance and the accepted text (which is in the document anyway). Never a prompt, a
key, a file path, the scope that was sent, or the work log. The model id is stored by default
(it is not a secret; it says which model wrote the text); a person who does not want it leaves
the model unset in Settings › Co-writer, and an outside assistant's model is unknown anyway
(empty).

**Other formats** (odt, rtf, md, html, txt, tex, pdf, png): authorship is dropped — the copy is
written from a clone without the store **and without the `_OptimoAI_` bookmarks** (they would
have become anchors in HTML/Markdown). A warning is logged, `file.save` / `file.saveAs` answer
`"authorshipDropped": true`, and the status line says "this format does not keep who wrote
what". The open document keeps everything.

### 8.4 Load repair (§6 as implemented, `wordcraft_doc::authorship::reconcile`)

Run at the end of every `.docx` read; one log line per repair. Only `_OptimoAI…` bookmarks (and Margin's `_MarginAI…`) are
ever touched (read leniently: the spike's `_MarginAI1` names count too; new marks are always
`_OptimoAI_<8 hex>`).

| Found | Done |
|---|---|
| Entry, bookmark with start and end in one story and container, start before end | Kept. Text compared on view (8.5), not changed |
| Entry, bookmark missing | Entry dropped |
| Entry, bookmark empty (collapsed, or only other markers inside) | Entry and both markers dropped |
| Start without end / end without start | Marker dropped (the reader already skips ends whose id has no start) |
| End before start, or start and end in different stories or table cells | Both markers dropped, entry dropped |
| Two bookmarks with one name (a pasted copy) | The first start and the first end after it are kept; the other markers go |
| Two entries with one name | The second entry dropped |
| `_OptimoAI_` bookmark with no entry (stale, or a file without the store) | Markers dropped; the text stays |
| Part malformed, another namespace or another root | Ignored and carried through as a foreign store, like any custom XML part; an unparseable part cannot be recognised as ours |
| More than 10,000 spans in a part, over-long names, authors, models, dates, texts | Capped / skipped; a span without text and without a 16-hex hash is skipped |

### 8.5 Who wrote what (`wordcraft_doc::authorship::attribution`)

- Per span, the live text inside the bookmark (no placeholders, no tracked deletions; a line
  feed between paragraphs) is compared with the stored text. Equal → all the AI's. Different →
  the common beginning and end are the AI's; the edited middle is compared character by
  character (longest common subsequence) when both sides are at most 400 characters, else it
  counts as the person's as a whole. Hash-only spans: the AI's as a whole, flagged `edited` when
  the hash no longer matches.
- Characters in a tracked deletion are not counted. A person's tracked insertion inside a span
  is the person's. Nested or overlapping spans: a character is the AI's if any span says so.
- **Pending suggestions** (tracked insertions by an assistant, not yet accepted) are **not AI
  text yet**: they are counted apart. The status bar shows them as "· n % pending"; the pill
  says "Pending suggestions not counted"; chips never include them. Percentages are whole
  numbers that add up to 100 (people = the rest), grouped by the assistant's name as written,
  shown without an `AI · ` prefix.
- Counted over the body, headers, footers, notes and text boxes; comments are not document text.

### 8.6 View (V1–V4, Optimo layout only; Classic unchanged)

`view.authorship` (View › Show Authorship; in the command field), `ViewState::authorship`, never
saved. While on: the pill at the top of the desk (legend People solid · each assistant dotted,
the pending note, Hide), accepted AI text with `assistant_tint` and a dotted `assistant`
underline on the page (`DisplayOptions::authorship`, computed once per document revision), a
chip "Claude 41 %" beside every paragraph with at least 10 % AI characters, and the split in the
status bar. Screenshots: `docs/mac/assistant-a5/`.

### 8.7 Round trips as built

`scripts/authorship-spike/run.sh` now also builds `f-feature.docx` with Optimo's own CLI (the
assistant's tracked insertions accepted by author) and round-trips it with the five candidates:
Optimo and upstream (`storytold/wordcraft` main `159b84f`) open+save and save after the scripted
edits keep all three spans, the store and the bookmarks (`spans=3 resolved=3`, the two edited
spans flagged by the hash; under Optimo the typing after span 3 lands after the bookmark end, so
that span still matches — under upstream, which has no such rule, it is flagged too). The
synthetic candidates B and E are not understood by the built reader (their store is in our
namespace but anchors nothing Optimo writes): their entries are dropped on save, the text is
untouched. **Word: still not measured** — not installed on this Mac; run the
script on a Mac with Word before relying on Word round trips. The only failure mode that matters
is a file Word or WordCraft refuses or repairs; losing marks is tolerable and visible (the
entries are dropped on load with a log line).
